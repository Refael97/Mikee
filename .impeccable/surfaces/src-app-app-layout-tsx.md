Scope: the whole Mikee app (Operate). Visitor: a small-business owner, phone between customers and laptop at night, approving Mikee's proposals and checking results.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight at Stripe Dashboard's craft level: an operational console that is calm around money. Refuses the editorial/cream/serif look and every themed world.

OWN-WORLD: White surfaces on a cool near-white shell, hairline borders (#ebeef1), one ink (#1a1f36), Mikee violet (#4B3FE0) for primary actions, links, selection and Mikee himself. Heebo in four weights, tabular numerals. 6px controls, 8px cards, 4px status badges with tinted grounds. Lucide icons at 1.75 stroke.

STORY: The owner sees what Mikee needs from her (proposals waiting), what happened (numbers with their history), and talks to him. Approve in one tap, never wonder where things are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Right-side (RTL start) sidebar: business switcher with Mikee's mark, nav with icons (Conversations, Results, Campaigns, Library, Business), settings/help/account pinned at the bottom. Content: page title row with actions on the end; on Conversations, a conversation list column and the chat column with the composer docked at the bottom.

FORM: canon (category standard), benchmark Stripe Dashboard. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
