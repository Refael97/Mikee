# Requests from public authorities for user data

Policy of ADRT GROUP LTD, the operator of Mikee and the controller of the data Mikee receives from Meta.
Adopted October 2026. Owner: Refael Levi.

1. **Legality review.** Every request from a public authority for personal data of Mikee's users (including
   data received from Meta) is reviewed before any answer, for its legal basis, the authority's jurisdiction
   and its scope. Requests without a valid legal basis are refused.
2. **Challenge.** A request that appears unlawful, overly broad or outside the authority's jurisdiction is
   challenged, with legal counsel, through the channels the law provides, and no data is disclosed while the
   challenge is pending unless a court orders otherwise.
3. **Data minimization.** When disclosure is legally required, only the minimum data needed to comply is
   disclosed, limited to the users, fields and period the request names. Access tokens and app secrets are
   never disclosed.
4. **Documentation.** Every request is recorded: date, authority, legal basis, the data asked for, the data
   disclosed (if any), the legal reasoning, and the people involved in the decision. Records are kept for at
   least five years.
5. **Notice.** Where the law allows, the affected business is told about the request before any disclosure.

As of adoption, no such request has been received.
