# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners of small businesses in Israel (the first, pilot client is a fashion brand with an online store) who are not marketers and have no time to manage Meta ads. They use Mikee on the phone between tasks and at the computer when they sit down to plan, equally. Hebrew, right to left. The job: "make the marketing of my business work, without me having to learn ad systems", while staying in control of every shekel and every public post.

## Product Purpose
Mikee is the business's marketer, in a conversation. The owner talks only with Mikee; behind him a team of specialist agents (copywriter, media buyer, creative director, social manager, analyst) does the work. Mikee learns the business (website, store, past campaigns, social), knows where it stands on its marketing path, proposes the next move, prepares campaigns, posts and media, and brings each one as a card to approve. Success: the owner approves good proposals in seconds, sees results in plain language, and the business grows within the monthly budget she set.

## Positioning
An AI marketer that acts, not a dashboard or an ad tool: it reads the real business data, thinks in a marketing plan, reaches out on its own, and executes on Meta, but nothing spends money or goes public without the owner's one-tap approval of that exact version, and the monthly budget is a hard ceiling enforced by the platform (and at Meta).

## Operating Context
- Conversations filed by topic; proposal cards (campaign, change, post, media, budget) approved in the chat.
- Mikee reaches out by himself: morning check, weekly and monthly summaries, reminders, fixes, next steps.
- Pages: conversations, results (a few numbers Mikee chose), campaigns across platforms, content library, the business (plan and what Mikee knows), settings and connectors, profile, help.
- Connected: Meta (Facebook, Instagram, ad account, pixel, catalog), website and store (Shopify/WooCommerce), Google Drive; Google Ads, TikTok, email and more are coming.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js on Railway with Postgres; Hebrew RTL; light and dark.
- Will serve many small businesses after the pilot (SaaS with plans: storage and AI limits per plan).
- The owner never sees ad-system jargon (ad sets, IDs, pixels, objectives).
- No invented numbers or scores; real numbers from the data only.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Mikee (מייקי). He speaks Hebrew, warm, brief, like a trusted colleague on WhatsApp.
- Writing rules: short direct sentences, no dashes to join clauses, no made-up percentage scores.
- The owner asked for a design that feels made by a person, with character, not "AI slop": no overly rounded corners, no pill labels, no generic SaaS look.
- Mikee has an own brand color (violet, #4B3FE0) and a character mark.

## Evidence on Hand
No customer testimonials, case studies, logos or real results yet (pilot stage). Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. The owner is in control: every action is a proposal she can read in seconds and approve with one tap.
2. Plain language over data: a few numbers that matter, explained, never a wall of metrics.
3. Mikee leads: he knows the plan, notices things and reaches out; the owner never has to know what to ask.
4. Trust is the product: budgets, approvals, privacy and security are visible and never surprising.
5. Real over generated: the business's own content and data come first.

## Visual Standard
The owner chose the category standard, played straight, at the craft level of the Stripe Dashboard: calm, precise, trustworthy around money, clear data. No themed "world", no costume; character comes from precision, Mikee's mark and his voice.
